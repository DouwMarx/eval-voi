The iteration loop (run a batch, compute health checks, change one thing,
re-run, compare) was executed three times on the full scenario set, plus a
sensitivity-guided deepening pass. What it taught, in decreasing order of
generality:

\paragraph{Format compliance is a solved problem; scale calibration is not.}
Every batch cleared the validity gates almost perfectly (JSON validity
$\geq 99.5\%$, constraint pass $\geq 99.5\%$, and 100\% of slots valid after
the single-retry rule) --- the final protocol's attempt-level validity of
\voiValidityRate{} saturates the $\geq 95\%$ target with no tuning ever
needed. All the difficulty lives in the numbers themselves: the dollar
parameters came back with cross-repeat spreads near or above 1.0 in every
protocol, while probabilities were reproducible to a few percent.

\paragraph{Two reasonable prompt fixes failed, informatively.} Iteration 2
(p002) required an explicit anchor-ratio statement (``roughly $3\times$ the
gearbox $B$'') before each dollar percentile: dollar spreads got
\emph{worse}, plausibly because choosing an anchor and a ratio adds free
variables instead of removing one. Iteration 3 (p003) required explicit
component arithmetic (a Fermi decomposition summed in the reasoning): the
model did the arithmetic faithfully, and the spread moved into the component
values. Both changes were reverted. The residual dollar noise appears to be
genuine epistemic uncertainty of the elicitor, not a formatting artifact ---
which is exactly what the repeats-as-noise-model design is meant to expose.

\paragraph{The ranking is noise-fragile at the rank level, stable at the tier
level.} Full-sweep variants correlate at
$\rho = \voiRhoProtoMin$--$\voiRhoProtoMax$; the rank-stability analysis of
the final run (Figure~\ref{fig:stability}) shows even top-3 scenarios are in
the top 10 in well under all draws. Claims should be made about tiers, not
adjacent ranks.

\paragraph{Deepening helped only marginally --- and taught a methods lesson.}
The sensitivity analysis pinned the top of the ranking on $C$ and $B$
(Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}), and a focused prompt variant (p004:
vendor-invoice pricing for $C$, single-dominant-component sizing for $B$) was
re-run on the top-quartile scenarios. At matched $k$, the targeted spreads
improved modestly ($C$: $0.96 \to 0.83$; $B$: $1.00 \to 0.88$) while an
untargeted parameter degraded by more ($K$: $0.80 \to 1.25$) --- with 17
scenarios, indistinguishable from noise. A first comparison had looked
dramatic and was an artifact of comparing 5-repeat ranges against 3-repeat
ranges; repeat-noise comparisons must be made at matched $k$. The one
intervention that reliably helped was buying more repeats: the final run
pools $k=\voiKUsed$.

\paragraph{Harness learnings.} The headless CLI's \texttt{--bare} isolation
mode is incompatible with OAuth credentials in this environment; equivalent
context isolation was achieved (and verified empirically) with disabled
tools, disabled setting sources, and a fixed system prompt. The
\texttt{--max-turns} flag of the spec no longer exists in the installed CLI;
with tools disabled, calls are single-turn by construction. The model wrapped
JSON in markdown fences from the very first smoke test, so fence-stripping
before parsing is load-bearing. Storing the CLI's full JSON envelope as the
raw response preserved cost and session metadata without schema changes. An
adversarial multi-agent review of the pipeline found (and led to fixes for)
two real defects: a replay guard that missed added repeats, and a
non-atomic elicitation write that could silently unbalance mixture weights
after a crash.

\paragraph{For v2.} Elicit dollar parameters on an explicit log-decade scale
or with retrieval grounding; model parameter correlations (the independence
assumption most distorts scenarios where stakes and costs co-vary); compare
elicitors (a larger model as a second rater) on the same protocol; and
validate the informativeness constraint's rejection behavior against a
signal-flip repair.
