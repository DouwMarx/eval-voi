# Fact-check of studies/sim2real/scenarios.json

Date: 2026-09-28. Method: every scenario's context paragraph and instrument was read; each specific claim (number, organisation, metric, cost) was traced to its catalog key in research/catalog.json and then to the primary source (arXiv full text, protocol PDF, journal page, price sheet). Sources were fetched and grepped directly where possible; where a web page was blocked the PDF was pulled via a mirror. WebSearch was unavailable, so claims that rested only on search snippets were verified from the primary document or removed.

Totals: 177 claims checked, 40 edits applied (35 substantive corrections or precision fixes, 5 pure removals of unverifiable claims; one further unverifiable pair of figures was dropped inside a correction), 15 scenarios and all titles unchanged.

VOI framing: all 15 scenarios pass. theta=1 is the hazardous property (unsafe behaviour mode present / AEB fails to brake for a crossing pedestrian), every instrument starts "Built from scratch and run once", every decision is delay-and-mitigate vs launch/release as planned, and the level in the title matches attributes.level.

## Remaining doubts

1. MSSBench size is internally inconsistent in the paper (abstract 1,820, body 1,960); the scenario now states both.
2. Veo nominal-task Pearson and MMRV appear only in Figure 4 (image); the text gives no number, so the scenario reports the OOD figures (MMRV 0.06, Pearson 0.86) and points to Fig. 4 for nominal.
3. Zhang et al. 2025 (Communications in Transportation Research) could not be read beyond title and author names; the AEB combined virtual-real claim rests on the title.
4. Amazon 2019 injury figures rest on the OnLabor summary of the SOC report; the SOC PDF returned 404.
5. NHTSA SGO reporting is confirmed indirectly (Waymo studies use SGO data); the nhtsa.gov page was blocked, so no incident count is given.
6. Euro NCAP AEB VRU protocol PDF was not retrieved; scenario names CPFA/CPNA/CPNC/CPLA are standard labels (CPNA-25, CPNC-50, CPLA-25 appear in the IIHS protocol) and the 2016 start date comes from the Wikipedia rating history.
7. ISO 10218 price (USD 244) is the A3 store price reported by The Robot Report; iso.org was not fetched.
8. Swiss Re "about 1,000 runs" is derived (13 vehicles x 81 average); the paper states the average, not the total.
9. ASIMOV-Agentic dataset component count (7) is an inference from the Hugging Face directory listing; the report does not state per-component counts.
