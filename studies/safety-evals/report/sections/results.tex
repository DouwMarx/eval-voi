\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\emph{Placement.} At the central estimate a random physical-AI evaluation is worth more per dollar than a random LLM evaluation with probability $A=\voiEtaACentral$, and the exact Mann-Whitney test separates the groups ($p_\mathrm{MW}=\voiEtaMWp$). % CHECK-DATA
On the maximum EVSI per dollar $\etamax$, every LLM evaluation ranks above every physical-AI one ($A=\voiEtaMaxACentral$, $p_\mathrm{MW}=\voiEtaMaxMWp$). % CHECK-DATA
$A$ is also computed on each Monte Carlo draw from the full set of evaluations. The draw-level value sits near one half (median \voiEtaAQMed, 90\% interval \voiEtaAQLo{} to \voiEtaAQHi) because on most draws many evaluations of both groups are worth exactly zero, and ties at zero count half. The central estimate is the comparison. % CHECK-DATA
The five highest efficiencies belong to LLM evaluations, led by \voiTopShort{} ($\eta=\voiTopEta$). The best physical-AI evaluations are the robot-command refusal suite ($\eta=\voiEtaScLLMRobotCommandRefusalSuites$) and ASIMOV agentic orchestration ($\eta=\voiEtaScASIMOVAgenticSafetyOrchestration$). % CHECK-DATA

\emph{Why: the stakes, not the cost.} The groups look alike on what an evaluation controls. Physical-AI against LLM medians: sensitivity \voiMedSPhysical{} and \voiMedSLLM, specificity \voiMedTPhysical{} and \voiMedTLLM, build cost \voiMedCBuildPhysical{} and \voiMedCBuildLLM, run cost \voiMedCRunPhysical{} and \voiMedCRunLLM, reuse count \voiMedNPhysical{} and \voiMedNLLM. They differ in the decision. The median stakes $B+K$ are \voiMedStakesLLM{} for an LLM release and \voiMedStakesPhysical{} for a physical-AI one, and the median stakes per evaluation dollar are \voiMedStakesPerCostLLM{} against \voiMedStakesPerCostPhysical, a factor of \voiStakesPerCostRatio. The median $\etamax$ differs by a similar factor (\voiMedEtaMaxLLM{} against \voiMedEtaMaxPhysical). % CHECK-DATA
Physical-AI priors are also higher (median $p$ \voiMedPPhysical{} against \voiMedPLLM), so for more of them the prior is already decisive. The result can change the decision for \voiChangesShareLLM{} of the LLM and \voiChangesSharePhysical{} of the physical-AI evaluations; for \voiNRespondsRegardlessPhysical{} of the \voiNPhysical{} physical-AI evaluations the developer responds regardless. There, the useful step is to mitigate first and evaluate the mitigated system. % CHECK-DATA

\emph{Scale.} EVSI and $\EVSImax$ grow in proportion to the stakes at a fixed threshold $K/(B+K)$. Physical-AI evaluations would draw level with LLM ones on $\etamax$ at about \voiEtaMaxRatio{} times today's stakes per decision. % CHECK-DATA

\emph{Break-even reuse.} Where one run is worth more than it costs to run, building pays fast: the median break-even reuse count at the central estimate is \voiNstarMedianPhysical{} decisions for physical-AI and \voiNstarMedianLLM{} for LLM evaluations, below one, against an elicited reuse count near \voiMedNPhysical. The median probability that the elicited reuse reaches it is \voiPPaysMedianPhysical{} (physical AI) and \voiPPaysMedianLLM{} (LLM). % CHECK-DATA

\emph{Robustness and fidelity.} The draws carry the disagreement among the elicitors. The median interquartile range of an evaluation's rank over draws is \voiRankIQRMedian{} of \voiNScenarios{} places (physical AI \voiRankIQRMedianPhysical, LLM \voiRankIQRMedianLLM): across that disagreement a rank moves by about \voiRankIQRMedian{} places, mostly between its positive-value rank and the shared rank of the tied zeros, so single ranks are not settled. % CHECK-DATA
The best physical-AI evaluation ranks in the top 5 on \voiPBestPhysicalTopFive{} of draws and in the top 10 on \voiPBestPhysicalTopTen{} (rank \voiBestPhysicalRankCentral{} at the central estimate). % CHECK-DATA
Averaged over evaluations, the uncertainty in $\eta$ correlates most with the uncertainty in $K$ (mean absolute Spearman \voiRhoK), and least with the cost parameters (\voiRhoCBuild{} for $C_\mathrm{build}$). The costs, the parameters the intuition is about, barely move the value. Across the \voiNLevel{} physical-AI evaluations, the Spearman correlation of fidelity level with the elicited Youden's index is $\voiLevelRhoJ$ and with cost $\voiLevelRhoC$: in this set, higher fidelity buys neither a more discriminating test nor a costlier one. % CHECK-DATA
