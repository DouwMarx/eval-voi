\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\emph{Elicited, not measured.} Every parameter is a language model's belief. LLM intervals are often too narrow~\citep{hobor2026bayesian}, and no evaluation publishes a sensitivity and specificity against real harm. The elicitors here are two Claude models with two repeats each; members trained on similar data may share knowledge and bias, and three of the LLM evaluations are Anthropic's own. A final ensemble of six models from six other developers is planned (\inextended{Section~\ref{sec:final}}). % CHECK-DATA
\emph{One result, one decision.} Each evaluation is reduced to one pass or fail result for one release decision. \emph{Independence.} The Monte Carlo draws each parameter independently, and each member's component independently per evaluation, so a member's systematic view is not carried through a draw. \emph{A snapshot.} The numbers describe present-day deployments; as robot fleets grow, the stakes and the reuse count of physical-AI evaluations grow too. \emph{Selection.} The groups come from different literatures by different rules, so the comparison is of the evaluations that exist, not of matched pairs.
